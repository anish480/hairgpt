Key Takeaways

  • Conversion is strong. 72.1% of engaged users (2+ messages) reach a routine recommendation — the core funnel is working.
  • Bounce is stable at ~23%. About 1 in 4 users sends a single message and leaves. Worth investigating what those first messages are — cold openers, test messages, or genuine drop-offs.
  • Peak was early July (Jul 5–12, 220+ sessions/day). Traffic softened ~18% in the second half, with Jul 22–23 the lowest stretch at 126–135/day.
  • Photo adoption is solid. 36.7% of sessions include a photo upload, driving richer hair analysis and likely higher-quality routines.
  • Almost entirely mobile. 92% of sessions come from phones (55% Android, 37% iPhone). Desktop is negligible — widget UX should remain mobile-first.
  • Deep conversations happen. 856 sessions (15.6%) went 7+ messages — these are likely your most invested users and potential repeat customers.
